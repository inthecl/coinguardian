Understand every indicator

A quick guide to the signals Coin Guardian watches for you, and how each one is read.

RSI

Measures the strength of buying vs. selling pressure on a scale of 0 to 100.

70+→Overbought, may fall
30 or below→Oversold, may rise
70/30 is the most common threshold, though some traders use a stricter 80/20.

Bollinger Bands

Shows how far price has moved from its average, using a band of expected range.

Touches upper band→Overbought, may fall
Touches lower band→Oversold, may rise
A 20-period moving average with bands at 2 standard deviations is the most common setup.

Moving Averages

A line that smooths price over time to reveal the overall trend direction.

Price above the line→Uptrend
Price below the line→Downtrend
SMA weighs every period equally. EMA weighs recent prices more, reacting faster to change.

Volume Spike

A sudden jump in trading volume, often a sign of a big price move ahead.

Sudden surge in volume→Big move likely
A spike of 2–3x the usual volume is a common trigger, though some set a higher bar.
